## 1. Purpose

UniFed is a university learning/social space. These guidelines keep it safe and respectful.

## 2. Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination. Debate ideas, not people.

## 3. Protect privacy

Do not post others' personal/confidential data (grades, contact, health) without consent.

## 4. Academic honesty

Submit your own work; credit sources; no contract cheating.

## 5. Authentic identity

Use your real university identity; no impersonation. Federation displays your actor identity.

## 6. Content standards

Keep content lawful and within university context. Flag harmful content.

## 7. Moderation

Universities moderate their instances; federated content is subject to `FEDERATION-POLICY-DRAFT.md`
and each instance's rules. Appeals via [MODERATION CONTACT].
