### 1. Definitions

"**Assigned IP**" means all intellectual property rights created by the Developer, alone or with
others, in connection with the Project (defined below), including:
- Source code, scripts, configuration, infrastructure-as-code (Terraform), CI/CD config;
- Documentation, architecture, designs, UI/UX;
- Database schema / migrations;
- Tests and technical documentation;
- Original inventions, designs, and work product;
- Any moral rights, to the extent assignable.

"**Project**" means the UniFed platform and related works, including the repository at
[REPO URL], and any task assigned by the Company.

### 2. Assignment

The Developer hereby **absolutely assigns** to the Company all right, title, and interest in the
Assigned IP, including all copyright, moral rights (where waivable), and neighbouring rights,
worldwide, together with the right to register and enforce them in the Company's name.

### 3. Pre-existing IP

The Developer retains ownership of Pre-existing IP listed in **Schedule 1**. To the extent
Pre-existing IP is incorporated into the Project, the Developer grants the Company a perpetual,
non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use it.

### 4. Third-party / open-source IP

The Developer confirms the Assigned IP does not improperly include third-party or open-source code
except as permitted by its licence. Where open-source components are used, the Company remains
bound by those licences.

### 5. Representations

The Developer represents that: (a) the Assigned IP is original; (b) it does not infringe any
third-party right; (c) the Developer has the power to assign; (d) no prior assignment conflicts.

### 6. Further assurances

The Developer shall sign further documents reasonably required to perfect the assignment.

### 7. Confidentiality

The Developer shall keep Confidential Information confidential, per [CONFIDENTIALITY TERMS].

### 8. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Developer consents to all acts in relation to the Assigned IP
that would otherwise infringe moral rights, and waives such rights where lawful.
