## 1. Who we are

UniFed ("**we**", "**UniFed**") operates a federated Digital University Operating System used by
partner universities including [UNIVERSITY LEGAL NAME]. For personal data you provide to a
university instance, the **university is the data controller** and UniFed acts as a
[PROCESSOR / JOINT CONTROLLER — CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL]. Contact: [UNIFED DPO EMAIL / ADDRESS].

## 2. Data we process

- Account & identity: name, email, username, role, credential material (hashed), MFA enrolment.
- Academic data: results, transcripts, assessments, assignments (where your university loads it).
- Profile & content: posts, comments, events, library activity, career information.
- Communications & metadata: messages, IP address, device/user-agent, session identifiers.
- Federated data: actor metadata shared with other universities via ActivityPub (see §6).

## 3. Legal basis

We process on the basis of [CONSENT / CONTRACT / LEGAL OBLIGATION — CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL],
including your university's legitimate interest in providing education. You may withdraw consent

## 4. How we use data

To authenticate you; provide the service; deliver academic and community features; federate with
other universities per your university's configuration; secure the platform; and meet legal
obligations.

## 5. Sharing

We share data only with: your university; other federation instances your university has enabled
(§6); and vetted sub-processors (hosting, email) under data-processing agreements. We do **not**
sell personal data.

## 6. Federation

UniFed uses ActivityPub to connect university instances. Federation copies actor metadata and
public/permitted content to other instances. **Federation does not transfer ownership of your
data.** You can request limiting or stopping federation via your university (see
`FEDERATION-POLICY-DRAFT.md`).

## 7. Your rights

Under NDPA 2023 you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability,
objection, and **withdraw consent**. To exercise them, contact [DSR CONTACT] or your university.
We respond within [TIMEFRAME]. Note: some deletion requests may not reach data already federated
to other instances — we will coordinate per the federation policy.

## 8. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed or required (see `DATA-RETENTION-POLICY-DRAFT.md`).
Academic records are retained per your university's policy.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed credentials, role-based access control, MFA, and the
remediations documented in our security assessment. No method is 100% secure.
