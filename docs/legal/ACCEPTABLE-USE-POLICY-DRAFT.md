# Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT — REQUIRES REVIEW BY QUALIFIED NIGERIAN LAWYER**

---

## 1. Scope

Applies to all users of a UniFed instance and federated interactions.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

- Unlawful content or activity under Nigerian law.
- Unauthorised access, scraping, credential theft, SSRF/abuse of federation endpoints.
- Harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination.
- Sharing of another's personal data without consent.
- Impersonation of a person, university, or instance.
- Circumventing security/rate limits/access controls.
- Uploading malware or harmful content.

## 3. Academic integrity

No cheating, unauthorised sharing of assessments, or falsification of records.

## 4. Federation conduct

Do not abuse federation (spam, fake actors, scraping other instances). Respect each instance's
federation policy and blocks/defederation.

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may lead to content removal, account suspension/deactivation, or instance
blocking/defederation, per `FEDERATION-POLICY-DRAFT.md` and university policy.
