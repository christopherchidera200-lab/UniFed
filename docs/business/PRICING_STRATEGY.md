## 1. Pricing philosophy

- **University pays**, student is free. Avoid per-student shock pricing for public unis (fee caps).
- Anchor on **annual institutional subscription**, use **students only as a tier metric**.
- **Pilot = subsidised/free** for validation (build reference + case study).
- Premium modules priced à-la-carte so unis self-select.
- All prices **ranges**; finalise after unit-economics telemetry + ADUN pilot feedback.

## 2. Recommended tiers (INDICATIVE annual, USD)

| Tier | Target | Included | Indicative annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | ADUN / first unis | Core OS, 1 node, community support | **$0–$10k** (subsidised) |
| **Starter** | Small/state unis (<15k students) | Core OS, capped users, email support | **$15k–$40k** |
| **Enterprise** | Large/federal unis | Core + premium-ready, SLA, SSO, integrations | **$40k–$120k** |
| **Federation Network** | Multi-node consortia | Managed cross-instance services, shared search | **$20k–$80k** add-on |
| **Premium modules** (à-la-carte, per uni/yr) | — | AI assistant · Advanced analytics · Career/Employer network · Research hub · White-glove support | **$5k–$30k** each |

## 3. Nigerian local pricing (indicative ₦, verify)

Convert at ₦1,500/$ (confirm): Starter ≈ **₦22.5M–₦60M/yr**; Enterprise ≈ **₦60M–₦180M/yr**.
Public-university budgets vary widely; TETFund/ITF interventions may co-fund. Confirm willingness
via ADUN pilot commercial discussion.

## 4. Per-student component (tier metric only)

- Use as a **fairness cap**, e.g. Starter includes up to 15k students, then **$1–$3/student-yr**
  overflow. Keeps small unis affordable, captures large.

## 5. Scenario-based pricing logic

- **Cost-plus (floor):** infra+support per uni (UNIT_ECONOMICS) × 3–5× gross target.
- **Value-based (anchor):** vs status-quo (portal+LMS+social+career tools) replacement saving.
- **Competitive:** below enterprise LMS (e.g., Moodle+hosting+portal glue) fully-loaded cost, above
  bare Moodle.

## 6. What NOT to do
- Do not price primarily per-student (unis resist variable student fees; political).
- Do not monetise student data (NDPA + trust).
- Do not discount below infra floor for small unis without min-commit.
