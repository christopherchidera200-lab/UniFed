# UniFed Nigeria — Pricing Strategy

> Nigerian market context: Nigerian universities are price-sensitive, procurement is slow, and
> FX (NGN/USD) volatility is real. Pricing must be **anchored in USD for investors** but
> **quotable in NGN for institutions**, with annual institutional licenses as the primary motion.
> All prices below are **recommended ranges**, not commitments. Validate against university IT
> budgets (typically ₦20M–₦200M/yr for ERP/portal equivalents).

---

## 1. Tiers

### Pilot / Validation — **subsidized or free**
- ADUN reference node: free during pilot in exchange for a case study + reference letter.
- Goal: prove retention, engagement, and federation before charging.
- **Price: $0 (pilot) → $15k/yr post-pilot conversion.**

### University Starter — **institutional**
- 1 node, core OS (academic records, student ID, social/connect, profile), standard support.
- **Range: $25k–$45k/yr** (≈ ₦40M–₦72M at ₦1,600/$, indicative).
- Best for: single-campus, <15k students.

### University Enterprise — **larger deployment**
- Multi-faculty, SSO/ICT integration, admin portal, priority support, custom integrations.
- **Range: $60k–$110k/yr**.
- Best for: federal/large state universities, >15k students.

### Federated Network — **federation services**
- Cross-university discovery, shared research/career, federation ops.
- **Range: $10k–$30k/yr** add-on per participating node, or revenue-share on marketplace/career.
- Justifies the ActivityPub moat.

### Premium Services (per-module, opt-in)
- AI tutoring/analytics: **$1–$3/active-user/yr** or bundled.
- Career/employer premium: transaction/placement fee (5–10% of placement value, capped).
- Research collaboration: institution subscription or per-grant.
- Support/SLA tiers: $5k–$25k/yr.

## 2. Recommended primary model
**Institutional annual license (Starter/Enterprise) + optional premium modules.**
Rationale: matches university budgeting cycles, high gross margin (see `UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`),
and decouples revenue from per-student volatility. Per-student pricing is a *secondary* lever
(e.g., $1–$3/active-student/yr) only where institutions prefer it.

## 3. Pricing guardrails
- **Never sell student personal data** — explicit ethical/legal red line.
- Avoid pure transaction依赖 on marketplace until liquidity exists.
- Index NGN quotes to FX quarterly to protect margin.
- Offer 2-year locked discounts (improves cash + retention).
- Free/subsidized for pioneer universities (ADUN) in exchange for references.

## 4. International (post-Nigeria) ranges
- Sub-Saharan peers (Ghana, Kenya, Uganda): $20k–$90k/yr by tier.
- MENA/India (later): $40k–$150k/yr by tier.

## 5. Strongest pricing strategy (verdict)
Anchor on **institutional annual license**, land ADUN free→paid, expand via federation network
effect, monetize premium AI/career/research modules once engagement is proven. This maximizes
gross margin and minimizes collection risk vs. per-student micropayments.
