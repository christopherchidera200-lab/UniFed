## Read-through
- **Still loss-making through Y3** in all scenarios — typical for infra/PLG HE SaaS pre-scale.
- Path to profitability requires ~50+ paid unis OR premium-module attach OR grant coverage.
- **Grants materially extend runway** (non-dilutive) — pursue aggressively (GRANT_READINESS).
- Implied cumulative 3-yr need: **~$3.3M (cons) / ~$4.1M (base) / ~$3.5M (agg)** incl. grants offset.

## Strongest pricing sensitivity
- At $35k avg × 50 paid unis = $1.75M/yr revenue ≈ covers Y3 base personnel alone → underscores
  **need for price uplift (Enterprise $40k–$120k) + premium modules** to reach contribution positive.
