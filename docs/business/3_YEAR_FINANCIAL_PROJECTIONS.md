# UniFed Nigeria — 3-Year Financial Projections

> Three scenarios (Conservative / Base / Aggressive). University counts and pricing trace to
> `PRICING_STRATEGY.md` and `UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`. **No revenue is asserted as booked** — these are
> targets the model must hit. Personnel/infra from `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md` + `CLOUD_COST_ANALYSIS.md`.

## Assumptions
- Avg selling price (blended institutional): Conservative $30k · Base $45k · Aggressive $65k.
- Active students per university (avg): 8,000.
- Annual contract, 85% renewal assumed from Year 2.
- Team ramps: Y1 lean → Y2 base+ → Y3 loaded.
- Infra scales per `CLOUD_COST_ANALYSIS.md` stage table.

## Reading the model
- **Conservative never reaches EBITDA positive by Y3** — slow procurement dominates.
- **Base turns EBITDA-positive in Y3** (~$30k) — requires disciplined cost + 25 universities.
- **Aggressive is cash-generative by Y2** — assumes strong federation land-and-expand.
- All scenarios need **external capital** (grant + pre-seed/seed) to bridge the Y1–Y2 gap.
- Infra stays <15% of revenue until Stage 4 — margin expands with scale.

## Runway note
Pre-seed ~$250–400k funds ~12–18 months at lean run-rate, enough to reach ADUN pilot + 1–2
paying institutions. Seed ~$1.5–2.5M funds to ~15–20 universities and EBITDA approach.
