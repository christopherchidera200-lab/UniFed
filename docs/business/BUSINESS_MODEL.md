# UniFed Nigeria — Business Model

> Evaluates 17 revenue mechanisms against the actual product (see `COVERAGE.md`, `SAD.md`) and the
> Nigerian/African university context. Models are classified by viability stage. **No student data
> monetization is recommended** (ethical + NDPA red line).

## Realistic NOW (pre-pilot)
1. **University SaaS licensing** — only post-pilot; not yet.
2. **Grants (non-dilutive)** — realistic NOW; funds build + pilot (see `GRANT_READINESS.md`).
3. **Professional implementation/support** — realistic NOW as a services revenue line for pioneer deployments.

## Realistic AFTER PILOT (ADUN proof)
4. **Institutional annual subscription** (Starter/Enterprise) — PRIMARY. High margin, budget-aligned.
5. **Per-student pricing** — secondary; $1–3/active-student/yr supplement.
6. **Premium university services** — admin portal, SSO, priority SLA.
7. **White-label deployments** — for consortia / government.
8. **Government/institutional contracts** — state/federal education ministries.

## Realistic AT SCALE (federation live)
9. **Federated Network services** — cross-node discovery/research/career add-on.
10. **Career/employer partnerships** — placement fees (5–10%, capped), employer subscriptions.
11. **Marketplace transaction fees** — only with liquidity; 3–8% capped.
12. **Creator economy revenue** — creator subscriptions / tips (small take-rate).
13. **AI premium services** — tutoring, analytics, $1–3/active-user/yr.
14. **Research collaboration services** — per-grant or institution subscription.
15. **Employer recruitment services** — talent access subscriptions.
16. **Sponsored opportunities** — sponsored research/career posts (clearly labeled, not native-ad intrusions).

## INAPPROPRIATE / RISKY
17. **Selling student personal data** — PROHIBITED. NDPA 2023 violation + trust destruction.
- Surveilling student activity for ad-targeting — risky (NDPA, ethics).
- Unlicensed financial products to students — regulatory risk.

## Recommended initial business model
**Institutional annual license (Starter/Enterprise) is the anchor.** Land ADUN free→paid as the
reference, expand via federation network effect, layer premium AI/career/research modules once
engagement is proven. Grants bridge the pre-revenue build phase. Per-student pricing is a
supplementary motion only where institutions prefer it.

## Why this model fits the architecture
Federation (ActivityPub) means **no central database** — each university owns its instance. This
maps perfectly to **per-institution licensing** (the university is the economic buyer) while
students remain free end-users (driving adoption/network effects). It aligns incentives: universities
pay for control + compliance; students get a free, portable identity.
