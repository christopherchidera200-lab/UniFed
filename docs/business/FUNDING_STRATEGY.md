# UniFed Nigeria — Funding & Investment Scenarios

> Capital allocation ranges are **estimates** derived from `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md` run-rates and
> `3_YEAR_FINANCIAL_PROJECTIONS.md`. No amount is a commitment; each should be re-derived at raise time.

## A. Pre-Seed (~$250k–$400k)
**Purpose:** complete M1 slice, deploy ADUN pilot, close security (STRIX F-04/05/06/07/11/12),
establish legal/IP (LICENSE file, IP assignment), NDPA groundwork, initial infra, founding team.
**Allocation:**
- Engineering (finish + pilot): 45%
- Security & compliance: 15%
- Legal/IP: 8%
- Infra (pilot): 4%
- G&A/runway buffer: 28%
**Milestone unlocked:** ADUN pilot live + 1–2 paying institutions LOI.

## B. Seed (~$1.5M–$2.5M)
**Purpose:** 3–5 universities, federation build, core domains (messaging, search, career, library,
events), team scale, sales/partnerships, AI foundation, support, security hardening.
**Allocation:**
- Engineering (federation + domains): 45%
- Sales/partnerships/university success: 18%
- Infra (Stage 2): 8%
- Security/compliance/DPO: 10%
- G&A: 19%
**Milestone unlocked:** 8–15 universities, EBITDA approach (Base case Y3).

## C. Scale / Series A (~$6M–$12M)
**Purpose:** 20–50 universities, national expansion, media/live-stream, AI premium, enterprise sales,
ops/SRE, international (Ghana/Kenya) entry.
**Allocation:**
- Engineering (scale + AI + media): 40%
- Infra (Stage 3–4): 18%
- Enterprise sales + partnerships: 17%
- Ops/SRE/Security: 15%
- G&A + buffer: 10%
**Milestone unlocked:** national footprint, EBITDA-positive, federation network effects.

## D. Strategic / Govt / Grant (non-dilutive, layered)
- **Grants** bridge pre-revenue build (see `GRANT_READINESS.md`).
- **Strategic** (EdTech, telecom, cloud credits) for distribution + infra discounts.
- **Govt/ministry** contracts for public-university rollout (slow, high-credibility).

## Recommendation
Raise **pre-seed now (grant + angel)** to reach the ADUN pilot (the single biggest de-risking event),
then **seed** on pilot evidence. Avoid scaling infra spend before the pilot validates retention.
