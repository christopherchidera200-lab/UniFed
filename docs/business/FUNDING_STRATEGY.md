## 3. Required evidence (general)

- Legal/IP (🟡 drafts; ❌ licence/assignments).

## 4. Readiness gaps blocking grants (priority)
1. **LICENSE + IP assignments** (legal/IP readiness 35).
2. **DPIA** (data-protection readiness 40).
3. **Signed ADUN pilot agreement** (pilot readiness 45).
4. **Live pilot/deployment** (staging not yet live).
5. **Impact measurement framework.**

## 5. Sequencing
- **Now:** close legal/security gaps; submit 1–2 aligned grants (digital education / DPI / cyber).
- **Post-pilot:** scale grants (research, AI, scale) + seed raise.
- **Scale:** government contracts + Series A.
